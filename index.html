We believe that every child, regardless of their location, deserves access to the magic of reading. Our program works tirelessly to bridge the gap between suburban resources and rural needs.

In Tennessee, ¨17% of kids who aren't on grade level reading by 3rd grade don’t end up graduating.¨

Our mission is to fix that.

Our Structural Pillars

We carefully curate and select K-12 literature that meets the specific needs of rural libraries.

We build bridges between local donors and underserved student populations.

We manage the heavy lifting, from sorting and packing to final delivery.
