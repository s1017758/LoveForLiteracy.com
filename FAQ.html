Club Questions

Do I need to bring my own books?
It’s not required, however we encourage any donations possible!
Do I need to participate in every fundraiser?
Everyone needs to participate in at least two fundraisers! However the fundraising team can and will be in charge of that.
How often do we meet?
Twice a month on Thursdays! Right after school for 40 min. However, during book drives it will be a 1 hour long.
Do I get service hours for this?
Yes! An attendance sheet will be at every meeting, and if you speak to our teacher sponsor separately they can sign off on your hours. Meetings where we pack, ship, or fundraise count, and at the end of the month the sponsor can sign off on your hours form based off attendance.
What will we actually do during meetings?
We’ll break into teams. Officers will run each team:
  • Sorting/packing run by VP
  • Grant list/fundraising run by treasurer
  • Posters/websites/social media run by the Secretary
What is the "Literature Engaging Activity"?
At the end of the day, our goal isn’t just to drop off boxes and check the mark. We want to make sure we actually make an impact. We’ll choose a donation school and host "Reading Circles" or "Bookmark Workshops" to get kids excited about literature.
How many students do we serve?
As many as possible! We target low income elementary schools. After our target of 4 schools per year, we could reach well over 300 kids annually.
What happens if we get more books than you can ship?
That's great! We’ll store them safely and use grants/fundraiser money to ship them later. Any remaining at the end of the year are saved for the next cycle or donated locally.
Whats my role in the club?
You’ll choose one of three teams (which you can rotate later):

• Creative: Socials, campaigns, and flyers.
• Logistics: Sorting and packing donations.
• Finance: Fundraising and Grant applications.
