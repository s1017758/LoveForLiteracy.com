Support the Mission

Your generosity is the fuel that keeps our delivery vans moving and our bookshelves stocked. In rural Knoxville, many children live miles away from the nearest public library, making your donations their primary gateway to new worlds. Whether you are gifting a single used book or contributing a financial grant, you are directly impacting a student's ability to succeed in the classroom and beyond. Together, we can ensure that "The End" of one chapter is just the beginning of a lifelong love for reading.

How You Can Help

  • Gently Used Books: We accept K-12 fiction and non-fiction titles.
  • Financial Grants: Funds go directly toward MediaMail shipping costs.
  • Corporate Sponsorship: Partner your business with our Target 4 schools.
  • Volunteer Time: Join us on our monthly sorting Saturdays in Knoxville.
  • Community Bins: Host a donation bin at your local place of business.
