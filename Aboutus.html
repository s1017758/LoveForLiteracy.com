Our Story

Every book has a story,
Every chapter has an end.
So Welcome to Love for Literacy's beginnings...

Love for Literacy was officially founded in 2026 by a dedicated team of Farragut students seeking to bridge the literacy gap within Tennessees´ community. What began as a conversation about the lack of diverse reading materials in rural and low-income school districts quickly transformed into a mission-driven club focused on real-world impact.

By mobilizing local youth and partnering with community leaders, we have built a sustainable network that sources, sorts, and delivers quality literature to underserved areas. Our teams mission is to ensure that no zip code, no income, and no school stops a child from unlocking their love for literacy.

Our Core Values

  • Equity: Equalizing access to knowledge across all zip codes.
  • Curation: Providing only the highest quality educational resources.
  • Integrity: Operating with 100% transparency in our logistics.
  • Youth Leadership: Empowering Farragut students to lead.
  • Sustainability: Building a long-term resource for our Tennessee branches.
